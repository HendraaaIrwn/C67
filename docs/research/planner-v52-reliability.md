# Travelants v5.2 itinerary reliability

## Outcome and scope

This investigation explains why new on-device itineraries can fail and proposes changes that help travellers recover while preserving selected places, timing constraints, and retry identity. It covers the native v5.2 creation flow, the shared v5.1 evaluator, and MapKit ETA acquisition. The v5.2 release batched opening-hours requests and retained the v5.1 evaluator. The local investigation covers the checked-out development revision `1f5ed76`. The suggested implementation is pending, with implementation owner and reviewers unassigned. [v5.2 release evidence](../tasks/planner-v52-release.md)

### Flexible departure can miss a feasible minute

The search starts with departures 30 minutes apart and adds candidates derived from time-window boundaries using preceding visit durations. Those initial boundary calculations omit travel time and earlier waiting. Route-informed shifted candidates are generated from completed schedules and use v5.1 anchors, mainly session and weather boundaries. A candidate rejected for waiting therefore cannot currently generate a repair at the relevant opening boundary. The main shortlist checks up to 24 candidates while it has no complete schedule and can stop at 12 checked candidates after finding one. The separate 500-candidate cap covers cumulative engine work. An exhausted generated set can yield `.conflicts`. [Candidate generation and search](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/PlannerV5/PlannerV5Search.swift) · [v5.1 anchors](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/PlannerV5/PlannerV51.swift)

The following synthetic executable check called the actual `PlannerV51` with `.hybridV52` preferences and a deterministic 12-minute ETA. The museum has a confirmed-in-fixture window of 09:00–09:30, a fixed 30-minute visit, a 09:40 trip deadline, and no final leg. Each case uses the same selected stop.

| Departure policy | Total waiting allowed | Observed outcome |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Fixed 08:36 | 10 min | Conflict after one ETA. Arrival 08:48 requires 12 minutes of waiting. |
| Fixed 08:36 | 15 min | Scheduled after one ETA. Arrival 08:48, visit 09:00–09:30, wait 12 minutes. |
| Flexible from 08:36 | 10 min | Conflict after checking departures 08:36, 09:00, 09:01, and 09:06. |
| Fixed 08:38 | 10 min | Scheduled after one ETA. Arrival 08:50, visit 09:00–09:30, wait 10 minutes. |

The fixed 08:38 result demonstrates that the flexible search missed a valid departure even with fast, deterministic ETA data. MapKit outages and provider limits are independent failure modes.

The same four cases reproduced through `PocketPlanningRepository` with the fixture hours entered as a traveller confirmation. The fixed 08:36 request with a 10-minute cap failed with `V51_SCHEDULE_CONFLICT` and `total_wait_exceeded`. The flexible request failed with `V41_ACCESS_CONFLICT` and `domain_exhausted` because the tested candidates collectively produced both `total_wait_exceeded` and `visit_outside_hours_or_entry`. The two feasible cases succeeded. The sampling gap can therefore reach the app as an apparent opening-hours conflict. [Repository planning path](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/PocketPlanningRepository.swift) · [Schedule failure mapping](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/V51ItineraryAdapter.swift)

This boundary matters in the default flow. `PlanningPreferences` initializes the total waiting budget to zero, and the creation presenter enables flexible departure for scheduling methods when the schedule source is unset. Without external AI consent, automatic scheduling explicitly disables semantic assistance and AI processing. A recovery based on existing time and route evidence serves that flow directly. [Planning preferences](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Domain/Planning/PlanningPreferences.swift) · [Creation presenter](../../apps/ios/Features/TripCreation/Presenter/TripCreationPresenter.swift)

### ETA failures and request interruption follow different paths

The route session caches by directed leg and departure minute for one command. It typically allows up to 72 provider calls and checks a 90-second search budget before candidate reservation or a new quote. MapKit also has a separate 25-second cancellation timer per call. An in-flight request can therefore outlast the search budget. The adapter recognizes MapKit throttling. Other ETA errors are collapsed to `route_unavailable` and cached as missing for that leg and minute during the session. The engine keeps its own missing-key set. [Route session](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/V41RouteSession.swift) · [MapKit adapter](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/PocketAppleServices.swift) · [Planner ETA lookup](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/PlannerV5/PlannerV5Evaluation.swift)

The repository preserves the same command when a job-level connection interruption or timeout leaves it `running`. A scheduling failure thrown by the ETA search is currently terminal for that job and the presenter clears its pending request. Route reservations persist before provider calls. A reconstructed route session starts with the persisted call count but builds fresh in-memory caches and search candidates. Resuming can spend its remaining budget on work that preceded the interruption. The domain copy for a budget failure promises more candidates on retry, while an unchanged restart can revisit the same shortlist. [Job state and persistence](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/PocketPlanningRepository.swift) · [Presenter handling of failed jobs](../../apps/ios/Features/Planning/Presenter/PlanningPresenter.swift) · [Budget message](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Domain/Planning/PlanningModels.swift)

The current conflict UI has a practical recovery gap. `V51_SCHEDULE_CONFLICT` can fall through to a generic retry. A `total_wait_exceeded` issue has no dedicated human-readable detail or link to the waiting field. The existing duration alternatives check up to two single-stop reductions and require an explicit or semantic duration or a fixed session for every stop. Common automatic plans using at least one local estimate can therefore produce no duration alternative. [Trip creation actions](../../apps/ios/Features/TripCreation/View/TripCreationView.swift) · [Issue messages](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Domain/Planning/PlanningScheduleAssessment.swift) · [Duration alternatives](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/V41ItineraryPlanner.swift)

Injecting `URLError(.timedOut)` into the single ETA call of the otherwise feasible fixed 08:36 case produced a terminal `failed` job with `V41_NO_SCHEDULE`, `unavailableETA`, and `eta:route_unavailable`. [ETA failure mapping](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/V41RouteSession.swift) · [Job failure handling](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/PocketPlanningRepository.swift)

## Reassessment of candidate search

The two departure shifts considered in the initial investigation can both fail. In a synthetic arrival function where later departures never arrive earlier, leaving at 08:36 or 08:38 still arrives at 08:48, leaving at 08:42 arrives at 08:50, and leaving at 08:48 arrives at 09:01. At the museum, the first shift still needs 12 minutes of waiting. The second shift finishes one minute after closing. Departures 08:42–08:47 are feasible. Use the observed boundary to guide multiple bounded candidates. Multiple opening windows, cumulative waiting across stops, and the endpoint can produce separate feasible departure intervals. ### Order of search under a shared ETA budget

A second disposable Swift replay compared three orderings across 192 deterministic inputs, 32 for each stop count from one to six. The generator used SplitMix64 with per-input seeds of `0xc670000 + stopCount × 32 + trial`. The fixture families included fixed and flexible departure, locked order, narrow access windows, reservations, variable directional traffic, unavailable legs, and confirmed duration-only conflicts. The fixture oracle independently found a complete schedule for 168 inputs. The three arms used the same native evaluator and a cap of 72 distinct synthetic provider quotes per input. Range-first tried up to four estimated interval midpoints before the baseline. Failure-only recovery ran the baseline first and tried those seeds only after a failure with remaining quota. Both arms checked each seed as a full itinerary. Every returned result passed independent replay. [Fixture oracle](../../packages/C67Backend/Tests/Starter/PlannerExtremeFixtures.swift)

| Strategy | Complete with noisy directional estimates | Complete with distance-only estimates | Median / p95 quotes with directional estimates |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Current v5.2 | 116 / 168 | 116 / 168 | 9 / 24 |
| Range-first with fallback | 127 / 168 | 120 / 168 | 6 / 23 |
| Failure-only recovery | 127 / 168 | 120 / 168 | 10 / 25 |

The directional estimates used the synthetic base times with independent errors or a systematic bias. The distance-only variant used four minutes plus the difference between synthetic stop positions. Reusing the exact same input corpus isolated sensitivity to the estimates. With noisy directional estimates, range-first worsened the worst-then-total check-in shortfall rank in three inputs and increased driving time in seven inputs that both strategies solved. With distance-only estimates it worsened check-in rank in six and increased driving time in seven. Failure-only recovery preserved every successful baseline result by construction. It added 65 quotes over the entire directional replay and 40 over the distance-only replay. None of the feasible failures in the generated baseline exhausted all 72 quotes. The result therefore measures recovery with spare budget and sensitivity to estimates, with live latency and completion rates still unmeasured.

In a separate adversarial six-stop fixture, each visit takes ten minutes, each travel leg takes one minute, departure is flexible from 09:00, order is locked, and the trip must finish by 15:30. The return ETA is available only for a departure on that leg at 13:36, a minute selected from the late end of the baseline's request order. The oracle identifies 12:30 as the only complete itinerary departure. The existing search found it within 72 quotes. Range-first spent part of the same budget on its initial seed and reached the cap without a complete result. Failure-only recovery would retain the original result in this case. This constructed example establishes a quota-ordering regression that the generated corpus did not expose.

The failure-only arm also recovered both museum failures above at 08:43. It made one additional quote after the original four in each case, with the same ten-minute waiting cap and visit duration.

### Estimate recovery candidates from already observed ETA

The next replay tested 384 inputs with the same one-to-six-stop workload generator, half with the original seed set and half with a new seed offset of `0x2a00000`. The reference found a complete schedule in 336 inputs. The baseline ran once per input. Each recovery arm started with a copy of the same exact-minute cache and could check at most four seeds, spend up to 12 new quotes, and use at most 72 quotes across the entire command. The distance-only arm used the earlier geometric proxy. The observed-ETA arm used the closest valid quote for the directed leg within 30 minutes of the predicted departure, with the distance proxy for gaps. Only exact-minute quotes could satisfy the validator. The mixed arm interleaved seeds from both models. Returned itineraries passed independent replay. ## Revised implementation sequence

The [implementation task](../tasks/planner-v52-eta-recovery.md) records the approved first release scope and its production-code retest on iPhone. That scope uses one frozen recovery list. The ranking refresh discussed here remains an experiment.

### 1. Correct outcome reporting and preflight

Run deterministic input and constraint checks before any provider calls when the required data is already available. Carry a bounded diagnostic from the evaluator with the failing leg, time, rule, and minute difference. Use a proven conflict status when supported by hard constraints or complete coverage. For sampled or interrupted searches, show what was checked and which evidence is missing. Invalid dates, unavailable ETA, exhausted search, and a confirmed constraint each need a distinct action.

For a fixed 08:36 departure in the example, show that the museum opens at 09:00, arrival is estimated at 08:48, and the itinerary needs 12 minutes against a 10-minute budget. Offer a user-approved move to 08:38, if a recheck confirms it, or an allowed-wait increase. The current five-minute UI step can offer 15 minutes. Keep explicit departure, wait, duration, last entry, reservations, order locks, all selected destinations, and return requirements in force until the traveller approves a change. Check each proposed edit independently and show its effect on the whole itinerary. Add a direct link from the issue to the affected control. [Waiting controls](../../apps/ios/Features/TripCreation/View/PocketTripOptionsView.swift) · [Whole-route validation](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/PlannerV5/PlannerV5Evaluation.swift)

### 2. Add a bounded recovery pass for failed searches

Keep the existing first pass and its selection policy. When it returns no complete schedule and still has usable time and ETA budget, choose a few untested seeds from plausible departure intervals around opening, last entry, check-in, the cumulative wait cap, and the return deadline. Prioritize using valid ETA already observed for the same directed leg near the proposed time, and use geometry as a fallback estimate. Refresh the seed ranking at most once when a rejected check supplies new usable evidence. Share the exact-minute quote cache, deadline, cancellation, and provider budget with the first pass. Skip recovery for proven conflicts or active throttling and pass temporary connectivity errors to the corresponding recovery action. Verify all legs through the existing evaluator. Changes to first-pass priority require a separate comparison that preserves successful baseline outcomes and schedule quality. [Adaptive time discretization](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/trsc.2019.0911?journalCode=trsc) · [Current v5.1 ranking](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/PlannerV5/PlannerV51.swift)

Use a valid lower bound or a known hard contradiction, such as visit durations alone exceeding the time window, for hard pruning. Use distance and nearby quotes for priority. Deduplicate matching quotes by directed leg, departure minute, transport and routing options, with bounded temporary retention. Before starting a recovery candidate, account for the full leg count and final endpoint. Keep checks for optional ranking improvements within the original budget. [Dumas et al. on pruning in time-window routing](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.43.2.367) · [Apple Maps service terms, Attachment 6](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/#attachment-6)

A minute-by-minute exhaustive solver for small deterministic fixtures provides an independent test oracle for the production heuristic. For six stops the 720 possible orders are tractable offline, but different actual departure minutes can produce distinct ETA requests for a single edge. Dynamic programming or branch and bound can be evaluated against the oracle. Any dominance rule must account for elapsed time and the remaining total waiting allowance, and any proof must state its model and coverage. A geometric ranking, traffic snapshots, or exhausting a shortlist cannot establish global infeasibility. OR-Tools exposes distinct statuses for invalid models, finding a valid result before optimization completes, timeout before a solution, and proven infeasibility. The same conceptual distinction fits the current Swift implementation. [Time-window routing model](https://developers.google.com/optimization/routing/vrptw) · [OR-Tools search statuses](https://developers.google.com/optimization/routing/routing_options#search_status)

Use an explicit search coverage outcome. When a sound lower bound or exhaustive evaluation of the supported deterministic domain proves conflict, say which rule blocks the plan. When verification stops or searches only sampled departures, explain that no complete plan was found in the routes and times checked. For requests with several eligible dates, preserve the outcome for each one. A conflict on the last date cannot establish a trip-wide conflict while another eligible date remains unverified. If one complete plan exists, identify it as checked and report access and route-estimate uncertainty separately. [Current search result types](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/PlannerV5/PlannerV5Models.swift) · [Date comparison](../../packages/C67Backend/Sources/Data/Planning/V41ItineraryPlanner.swift) · [OR-Tools search statuses](https://developers.google.com/optimization/routing/routing_options#search_status)

### 3. Make provider recovery resumable

Give ETA timeout, connectivity loss, throttling, definitive no-route responses when identifiable, and invalid quote data distinct reasons. Use the remaining end-to-end time as a ceiling for the next provider call. Coalesce duplicate in-flight requests and test pacing across consecutive planning attempts. Apply bounded backoff with jitter at a single layer for genuinely transient failures and use a cooldown for throttle responses. Resume the same command on connection recovery when possible. Persist only necessary command identity, counters, selected constraints and recoverable search progress whose storage is permitted. On resume, avoid blindly starting from the same shortlist. Refresh missing or expired evidence before presenting a checked result. [Apple MKDirections throttling](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/mapkit/mkdirections.md) · [AWS retry and backoff practice](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter/) · [Apple Maps service terms, Attachment 6](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/#attachment-6)

Apple documents native MapKit throttling for too many requests in a short period and gives no fixed numerical quota on the `MKDirections` page. Base pacing on measured bursts and throttles on physical devices. Apple Maps Server API can batch one origin and departure time with up to ten destinations, with a separate published shared quota for Server API and MapKit JS. That is an experiment if the native path remains constrained after measuring demand. Moving ETA calls to the server introduces per-leg location transfers, credential and quota ownership, and a network dependency. [Native MapKit behavior](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/mapkit/mkdirections.md) · [Server ETA endpoint](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applemapsserverapi/-v1-etas?language=_8) · [Server API quotas](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applemapsserverapi?changes=latest_major)

## Acceptance and verification

For a subsequent implementation:

- Reproduce the four synthetic museum cases. The repaired flexible search should produce a complete, rechecked plan with zero edits to the wait cap. Fixed departure should preserve the conflict until the user approves a valid edit.
- Include the time-dependent arrival counterexample, the pessimistic-proxy case, the six-stop buffer trade-off, and the rare return ETA. Match the baseline ETA cap, compare completion among oracle-feasible cases, and measure quotes to first complete result separately from total quotes. Preserve the existing quality criteria and check that no returned result violates a hard constraint.
- Retain the original successful plan in the late-return adversarial fixture. Evaluate recovery with spare budget and behavior when that budget is already exhausted. For live evaluation, compare the same public itineraries, device, and approximate request conditions. Record p95 wait and success separately for normal searches and recovered failures.
- Compare distance, nearby ETA, and one refresh on both seed sets and on device. Check that an earlier ETA is used only to prioritize candidates and that every scheduled leg has a valid quote for its actual departure minute.
- Cover cumulative waiting across two stops, exact cutoff and deadline boundaries, multiple windows, locked order and endpoint constraints, reservation check-in, unknown or regular access, and changed travel time after a proposed departure shift. Compare the solver with the exact small-fixture oracle.
- Inject throttling, timeout, disconnect, missing ETA, and cancellation. Check that a valid complete result remains available after a later search interruption, an unavailable route never becomes a verified leg, and resume preserves identity and progress without a retry loop.
